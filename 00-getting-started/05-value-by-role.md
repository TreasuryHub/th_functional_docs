# Why TreasuryHub: value by role

> **See what changes for your job, not just what the platform does**
>
> **Why it matters**
> - **Find your three outcomes fast**: each role below names what moves, with the pages that deliver it.
> - **Agree on scope before you start**: the whole finance function is covered by one layer, so nobody is left integrating around the edges.

> **Who this is for:** anyone deciding whether, and how, TreasuryHub earns its place in your week.

## Overview
The same layer looks different depending on where you sit. This page states what changes for each
role, and links straight to the screen that does it.

## CFO
**From** waiting for month-end to know where the business stands **to** a real-time view of cash,
margins and risk, ready for the board.

- A consolidated cash position across every bank, currency and entity — [Home: Cash Position](../01-home/home-dashboard.md).
- Board and investor packs built from reconciled data — [Build Your Own Report](../06-reporting/build-your-own.md).
- Every treasury risk on one screen — [Risk Cockpit](../06-reporting/risk-management/risk-cockpit.md).

## Treasurer
**From** daily operations with portals, files and balances **to** strategic capital and liquidity
planning.

- Every account in one live feed, with no portal logins — [Integrations: Overview](../02-integrations/overview.md).
- Funding gaps visible weeks ahead — [Cash Forecast](../06-reporting/cash-forecast.md).
- Payments from every bank through one governed workflow — [Payments: Overview](../05-payments/overview.md).

## Controller
**From** manual reconciliations and an endless close **to** a continuous close, with evidence on
every entry.

- Most items matched automatically, leaving only real exceptions — [Reconciliation: Overview](../04-reconciliation/overview.md).
- Journals drafted, approved and exported — [Journal Entries](../11-accounting/journal-entries.md).
- "How was this reconciled?" answered in one click — [Conciliations](../04-reconciliation/conciliations.md).

## Risk manager
**From** reactive compliance and late surprises **to** proactive, continuous risk management.

- Currency exposure and hedge coverage, live — [FX Risk](../06-reporting/risk-management/fx-risk.md).
- Counterparty deterioration before it becomes a loss — [Credit Risk](../06-reporting/risk-management/credit-risk.md).
- Limit breaches that reach you, not a report — [Alerts](../08-alerts/alerts.md).

## FP&A
**From** days spent collecting and cleaning data **to** insight and scenarios in minutes.

- Plain answers straight from consolidated data — [Datalake Reports](../06-reporting/datalake-reports.md).
- Forecast accuracy measured each cycle — [Actual vs Forecast](../06-reporting/actual-vs-forecast.md).
- Liquidity stress-tested before the market does it — [Scenarios (What-If)](../06-reporting/scenarios-what-if.md).

## Treasury operations
**From** chasing files, formats and missing statements **to** a feed that runs itself and tells you
when it does not.

- Completeness confirmed before anyone relies on the numbers — [Ingestion Activity](../02-integrations/ingestion-activity.md).
- A bank format change absorbed without losing a day — [AI Extraction](../02-integrations/ai-extraction.md).
- Payments built in one pass, to validated payees only — [Creating a Payment](../05-payments/creating-a-payment.md).

## IT
**From** building and maintaining bank integrations **to** connecting systems and leaving them
alone.

- New integrations in days, kept working by AI — [Third-party APIs & API Agent Integrator](../02-integrations/third-party-apis.md).
- Systems connected without sharing anyone's login — [Access Tokens](../10-admin-console/access-tokens.md).
- Events reacted to instantly, broken feeds spotted early — [Webhooks & Integration Status](../02-integrations/webhooks-and-status.md).

## Auditor and compliance
**From** sampling and reconstructing **to** an immutable record that answers the question directly.

- Any transaction followed end to end — [End-to-End Audit Trail](../11-accounting/audit-trail.md).
- Segregation of duties enforced, not asserted — [Roles & Permissions](04-roles-and-permissions.md).
- Regulator-ready reports from data you already hold — [Regulatory & Compliance Reports](../06-reporting/regulatory-compliance.md).

## Who runs on it
- **Growth companies & SMEs** — one finance layer from the first bank account, without a treasury department to run it.
- **Corporates & groups** — every entity, bank and currency consolidated, with governance that holds across the group.
- **Fintechs & PSPs** — high-volume reconciliation against providers, and fees checked against contract.
- **Banks & central banks** — multi-tenant and white-label from day one, with ALM and regulatory reporting built in.
